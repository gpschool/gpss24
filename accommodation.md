The University of Manchester has [this page](https://www.alliancembs.manchester.ac.uk/contact/hotel-bookings/) with special discounts at two hotels. 

Other options in walking distance include the [Maldron Hotel](https://goo.gl/maps/XpC1DJFcWQ5VTsgH8), the [Premier Inn on Princess Street](https://goo.gl/maps/fPFaisoUtDEdSYbD9) or the [Holiday Inn on Oxford Road](https://goo.gl/maps/kNB6gkKtruZywrxY7). 

There are many options, but those three are probably the most convenient after the Hyatt.
 
If somewhere near Piccadilly station is convenient, there are the following options:
 
[Manchester Piccadilly Hotel](https://goo.gl/maps/EgkL1LcfzH5kQUJw9) <br>
[DoubleTree by Hilton](https://goo.gl/maps/dRYzJxKk2htWDYqU7)<br>
[Native Manchester](https://goo.gl/maps/LGMwb1bgjLM64g7z6)<br>
[Holiday Inn City Centre](https://goo.gl/maps/gtuW5XmfvtJ9Cg2g6)<br>
[Malmaison](https://goo.gl/maps/2pbJ2SQXc6So6m647)<br>
[ABode](https://goo.gl/maps/Zbh3pV3S2B2xgVTb6)

The Summer School does not endorse any of these accommodations. Manchester is a big city and there are plenty of offers available.
